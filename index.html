Sharpening layer

No sharpening layer.

White: selected edges · grey: partial selection · black: protected. Amount changes sharpening strength, not this selection.

Paint the mask

Paint with one finger, Pencil or mouse. Two fingers pan / zoom. Restore removes painted exclusions; it does not select new edges.

Amount 0–200 gives subtle to moderate sharpening; 200–500 adds strong edge contrast. Use layer opacity to blend the result. Suppression smooths texture before finding edges. Threshold rejects weak edges. Width expands their outlines; Feather softens the mask. Fine / medium / coarse set sharpening strength by scale. Apply keeps an editable layer; Cancel discards this preview. Inspect at 100% before exporting.
